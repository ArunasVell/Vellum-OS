Your reader, before the device arrives

This runs the real Vellum code, compiled for the browser: the same fonts, layout and page turns the firmware draws on the X4 Pro.

How to use it

Next / Prev
The X4 Pro's side buttons: turn pages, or move the highlight on the home, library and settings screens.
OK
The power button: opens the highlighted book or screen. While reading, it opens the book's contents: jump to a chapter, bookmark the page, go back to a bookmark, or change the front light. Hold it for a second to put the reader to sleep; any button wakes it. The reader also sleeps by itself after 10 idle minutes (Settings › Sleep after).
Square key
Home: back to the home screen from anywhere.
Touch
The screen is a touch screen. Tap a book, link or setting to open or change it. While reading, tap the right third or swipe left to go forward, tap the left third or swipe right to go back, and tap the middle for the contents. Swipe the library or the contents to see more. With a mouse, click to tap and drag to swipe.
Hold a word
Keep the mouse button down on a word for a moment: its meaning shows at the bottom, from a dictionary you added (below). Highlight underlines it; tap another word on the page to underline the whole passage up to it. Highlights are listed in the contents, and holding a highlighted word offers to remove it. Without touch: contents › Look up a word, then the side buttons pick a line and a word.
Keyboard
Arrow keys, Space, Enter (OK), H (Home)

Your own books, fonts and dictionaries

Add books (EPUB, FB2 or .fb2.zip, TXT, HTML) and they appear in the library, just as books copied to the reader's SD card will. Font files (.ttf or .otf, for example from Google Fonts) become choices in Settings › Menu font and Reading › Book font. StarDict dictionaries (the .ifo, .idx and .dict or .dict.dz files together) let you hold a word to look it up; a name starting with its language, such as en-wordnet.ifo, matches it to books in that language. Drag them onto the screen, or choose them here. Books, settings and your place in each book stay in this browser for your next visit.

On the reader, Add books on the home screen opens its own Wi-Fi network and shows two codes: scan the first to join, the second to open the upload page on your phone. Open it here too, then add books: they arrive on the reader's screen the way they would from a phone.

Highlights, as the phone sees them

While Add books is open, the phone page lists your highlights by book. Add a note to one here and it shows under the highlight in the reader's contents once you press Home.

What to look for

  • Most page turns use a fast refresh with no flash.
  • After 12 fast turns, the next one is a full refresh: the screen flashes once to clear ghosting, as real e-ink does. Page back and forth to see it.
  • Text is set in Literata, a typeface designed for reading on screens, justified with real kerning. Long words are hyphenated so lines stay evenly spaced.
  • The home screen shows the book you are reading and where you are in it. Open another book and come back: each one remembers its page.
  • Settings has English or Lithuanian menus, a 24-hour or 12-hour clock, and how pages look: text size, line and word spacing, margins, alignment and hyphenation. A preview shows each change as you make it.
  • The time, page number and battery sit on one slim line at the very bottom, so more text fits on each page.

Send a book to another Vellum

Readers nearby can pass books to each other directly, with no Wi-Fi network or password. To try it, open this page in a second browser tab: that tab is the other reader. On one, open Add books. On the other, open a book you added, press OK for its contents, and choose Send to another Vellum.

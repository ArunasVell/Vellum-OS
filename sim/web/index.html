What to look for

  • Most page turns use a fast refresh with no flash.
  • After 12 fast turns, the next one is a full refresh: the screen flashes once to clear ghosting, as real e-ink does. Page back and forth to see it.
  • Text is set in Literata, a typeface designed for reading on screens, justified with real kerning. Long words are hyphenated so lines stay evenly spaced.
  • The home screen shows the book you are reading and where you are in it. Open another book and come back: each one remembers its page.
  • Settings has English or Lithuanian menus, a 24-hour or 12-hour clock, and how pages look: text size, line and word spacing, margins, alignment and hyphenation. A preview shows each change as you make it.
  • The time, page number and battery sit on one slim line at the very bottom, so more text fits on each page.

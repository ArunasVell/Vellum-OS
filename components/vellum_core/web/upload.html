Add books

Choose books on this phone or computer: EPUB, Kindle (MOBI, AZW3), PDF, FB2, Word (.docx), RTF, Markdown, TXT, HTML or comics (.cbz). They go straight onto the reader's SD card. Kindle books bought from Amazon are locked and cannot be read, and neither can PDFs that are only pictures of pages. Fonts (.ttf or .otf) work too: pick them in Settings › Menu font or Reading › Book font. So do StarDict dictionaries (.ifo, .idx and .dict or .dict.dz, all three): hold a word while reading to look it up.
